## 1. Situation: The 18-Year Gap and the Next Decade

I spent 18 years at Google, and I loved most of it. But when my time there
finally ended, I discovered that I had made no deliberate plans for my technical
work life after Google, and I had very little I could publicly show for those
two decades of work.

The public records I have are sparse: early contributions to Google Web Toolkit
(GWT) around input validation, and a few blog posts on media performance
classes. Behind corporate confidentiality and proprietary internal
infrastructure (Borg, Piper, Spanner), I designed and operated large-scale
distributed systems, but none of that code or architectural history can leave
the building. While I invested heavily in utilizing AI during my final year at
Google, that effort was 100% internally focused. When I walked out the door, I
left without any public records of AI work.

Meanwhile, the software industry underwent a structural phase change.

Coding alone is
[no longer a scarce or highly valuable skill](https://showmetheinference.com/posts/coding-like-quilting/).
Software engineering still is, but the real demand has shifted decisively toward
engineers who know how to build, operate, and govern modern AI systems.

I intend to remain competitive and productive in this industry for another
decade of high-impact work. To force the issue, I spent
[$5,000 of my own money on an ASUS Ascent GX10 workstation](https://showmetheinference.com/posts/why-i-spent-5000-on-hardware-instead-of-tokens/).
That machine is my forcing function: it compels me to master AI systems
engineering in a budgeted, disciplined, and realistic environment to solve real
problems. Most of the immediate problems I tackle will be code-centric, but AI
reaches far beyond code generation. My objective is to participate actively in
making AI generally useful, reliable, and production-ready.

## 2. Intent

### Purpose

To decisively demonstrate personal mastery across three core engineering
disciplines:

1. **Inference Engineering**: Running, profiling, and optimizing local
   foundation models on bare-metal silicon.
2. **Multi-Agent Orchestration**: Coordinating deterministic, structured
   multi-agent systems that solve complex, multi-step engineering tasks.
3. **Local Sovereign AI**: Operating a secure, cost-controlled, and
   self-sufficient local compute cluster minimizing use of closed commercial
   cloud APIs.

### Method

I will build an authentic professional network, verifiable public credentials,
and a durable reputation by doing real work on real silicon and sharing the
empirical truth.

We execute actual production workloads on local hardware, capture raw telemetry,
and publish reproducible findings, configurations, and repositories. We do not
publish speculative think-pieces or regurgitated documentation.

### End State

My technical competency, architectural depth, and systems leadership are visibly
demonstrated through
**[showmetheinference.com](https://showmetheinference.com)** and associated
public GitHub repositories, firmly positioning me for Staff-level and senior
engineering and leadership roles in the AI systems ecosystem.

## 3. Divestments (What We Will Not Do)

Focus requires explicit subtraction. To avoid diffuse effort and superficial
outputs, we enforce two strict boundaries:

1. **No Toy Demos**: We will not build generic chatbots, trivial
   prompt-wrappers, or synthetic toy benchmarks. Every workload executed across
   our systems must represent an actual production task, a genuine
   infrastructure challenge, or an empirical stress test.
2. **No Agent Ghostwriting**: Autonomous agents will profile runtimes, gather
   telemetry, run benchmark suites, and draft technical dossiers. However, every
   public dispatch will originate in my authentic voice, reflect my lived
   engineering perspective, and be shaped in disciplined collaboration with my
   staff. Readers must know they are reading my analysis, backed by verifiable
   receipts.

## 5. Invariants (The Non-Negotiables)

Four operational invariants govern all work across this portfolio:

1. **Empirical Receipts Over Assertions**: Every blog post, whitepaper, and case
   study must be backed by reproducible configs, timeseries graphs, and raw
   telemetry captured directly from the machines.
2. **Strict Cost Containment**: All hardware, token, power, and infrastructure
   expenses will be measured, budgeted, and transparently reported. We
   prioritize high-efficiency local compute over open-ended cloud spend.
3. **Continuous Staff Evolution**: Our multi-agent staff organization will
   evolve iteratively alongside the workloads it executes. We expand
   organizational capability concurrently with operational demand through
   continuous reflection and After-Action Reviews (AAR).
4. **Homelab & Personal Security**: The security of the local homelab
   environment will be continuously maintained. No experimental agentic
   workflow, network exposure, or credential handling will ever put personal or
   operational security at risk.
